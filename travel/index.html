Travel

Family road trips from Austin with maps, national parks, stops, hotels, the best season to go and live forecasts.

  • 8 days

    Southwest Family Road Trip

    Austin to the Grand Canyon, Page, Antelope Canyon and White Sands, and back.

    National parks: Grand Canyon · White Sands · Petrified Forest · Carlsbad Caverns (optional)

    Best season: April–May and October
  • 8 days

    Big Bend & West Texas Parks Family Road Trip

    A loop from Austin to Big Bend, Fort Davis and the McDonald Observatory, White Sands, Carlsbad Caverns and Guadalupe Mountains.

    National parks: Big Bend · White Sands · Carlsbad Caverns · Guadalupe Mountains, plus Fort Davis and Chamizal

    Best season: October–November and February–April (too hot June–August)
  • 10 days

    Colorado Parks Family Road Trip

    A loop from Austin to Great Sand Dunes, Black Canyon of the Gunnison, the Million Dollar Highway and Mesa Verde, home through Taos and Santa Fe.

    National parks: Great Sand Dunes · Black Canyon of the Gunnison · Mesa Verde, plus Capulin Volcano, Curecanti, Bandelier and Pecos

    Best season: June and September
  • 10 days

    Yellowstone & Grand Teton Family Road Trip

    Austin to Grand Teton and two full days in Yellowstone, home by Cody, the Thermopolis hot springs and Cheyenne.

    National parks: Grand Teton · Yellowstone, plus Capulin Volcano and Garden of the Gods

    Best season: mid-June to mid-July and September (most Yellowstone roads close Nov–Apr)
  • 6 days

    Destin & Gulf Coast Family Road Trip

    Austin to New Orleans and the white-sand beaches of Destin and 30A, and back.

    Gulf Islands National Seashore · Jean Lafitte NHP · USS Alabama · Space Center Houston

    Best season: late April–May and October
  • 9 days

    Great Smoky Mountains Family Road Trip

    A loop from Austin through Hot Springs, Memphis, Nashville and Mammoth Cave to the Smokies, and home by Chattanooga and Vicksburg.

    National parks: Hot Springs · Mammoth Cave · Great Smoky Mountains, plus Vicksburg, Chickamauga & Chattanooga and Little River Canyon

    Best season: mid-to-late October (fall colors) and May (wildflowers)
